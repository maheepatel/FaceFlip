## 1. Custom image FaceFlip (the "make your own deck" mode)

Let anyone build a FaceFlip game from their own pictures.

- Upload a few images, for example NFTs from a project you hold or love.
- Each image is cut into 4 quarters, and those quarters become the sides of the blocks.
- The cards are the full images, or mixes of quarters from different images, and players race to rebuild them.
- Share the custom game with a link so friends or a whole community can play it.

### Web3 touch (later)
- Connect a wallet and pull NFT images straight from a collection.
- A project could launch a branded FaceFlip for its holders.
- Possible extras: holder-only rooms, leaderboards per collection, and rewards for winners.

### Matching note
This still uses the current exact matching method: each slot is stored as "which picture and which way it is turned" and compared directly, never by comparing pixels. Custom quarters only need a fixed upright direction so a turned quarter counts as wrong.

## 2. Kido: a collection of kids' and fun games

**Kido** (or **Kidi**) is a small, funny brand for a collection of browser games, each inspired by an offline game: toys, board games, party games and playground games.

- FaceFlip is game #1.
- Each new game takes a physical or offline game, studies its real rules, and rebuilds it as an original digital game with its own art and names.
- The same base for all of them: works on phone, tablet and desktop, bots to play against, online 1v1 with a link, same screen mode, and simple modes for little kids.
- One home page that lists all the Kido games.

### Name options
- **Kido** (first choice)
- **Kidi**
- Others to consider: Kiddo Box, Giggly, Bonk

## 3. Desi 90s nostalgia games for Kido

Kido should lean into the desi games Indian kids played in the 80s and 90s: street games, tiffin-break games, train-journey games and festival games. They bring back nostalgia for adults and are new to today's kids. Parents and kids can play together, which is the hook.

The rule stays the same as FaceFlip: these are folk and traditional games, so we rebuild the rules in our own style with original art. We do not copy any branded board game, toy or video game.

### Street and playground games
| Game | What it is | How it works online |
|---|---|---|
| **Kanche** (marbles) | Flick your marble to hit others out of the circle | Drag back and release to flick, physics shots, 1v1 turns |
| **Lattu** (spinning top) | Wind the string, throw the top, keep it spinning longest | Swipe to throw, tap in rhythm to keep it alive, knock opponents' tops |
| **Pithu / Lagori** (seven stones) | Knock down a stack of 7 stones, rebuild it before you get tagged | Team game: one side aims the ball, the other races to restack |
| **Gilli Danda** | Flip the gilli up and strike it as far as you can | Timing tap to flip, swipe to hit, distance scoring |
| **Stapu** (hopscotch) | Throw a stone into a box and hop the grid | Rhythm and balance game for little kids |
| **Chain / Pakdam Pakdai, Chhupan Chhupai, Kho Kho** | Tag and hide and seek games | Simple top-down chase games for 2 to 4 players |

### Indoor and tiffin-break games
| Game | What it is | How it works online |
|---|---|---|
| **Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi** | Folded chits; the Sipahi must guess who the Chor is | Perfect for online rooms with 4 friends and a link |
| **Name Place Animal Thing** | Pick a letter, everyone fills the 4 columns fast | Party game for any number of players, votes on answers |
| **Book Cricket** | Open a book at random; the last digit of the page is your runs | Flip-the-book animation, team scorecards, quick 2 over matches |
| **Pen Fight** | Flick your pen to knock the other pen off the desk | Physics duel on a school desk, great for 1v1 online |
| **Chidiya Udd** | Lift your finger only if the thing can fly | Fast reflex game, kids vs parents |
| **Antakshari** | Sing a song starting with the last letter of the previous one | Turn timer and letter tracker for family voice calls |
| **Pitthu style paper games: Dots and Boxes, Tic Tac Toe, Bingo** | Back-of-the-notebook games | Classic notebook paper look, ruled lines and pencil marks |

### Board and table games
| Game | What it is | How it works online |
|---|---|---|
| **Saanp Seedi** (snakes and ladders) | The ancient Indian dice race | Desi illustrated board, festive themes |
| **Ludo / Pachisi / Chaupar** | Traditional dice and token race | Online 4 player rooms with bots filling empty seats |
| **Carrom** | Strike the coins into the pockets | Aim and power flick, the queen and cover rule |
| **Pallanguzhi / Chowka Bhara** | South Indian seed sowing and dice games | Teaches counting, great for kids |
| **Trading game** (like the 90s "business" board games) | Buy cities, collect rent | Our own board with Indian cities, rules our own |
| **Trump cards** | Compare stats on cards, highest number wins | Our own card sets: desi foods, animals, Indian cities |

### Festival games
- **Patang** (kite flying for Makar Sankranti): cut the other kite's string, 1v1 online.
- **Holi colour splash** and **Diwali rangoli** puzzle games for festival seasons.
- **Dahi Handi** human pyramid stacking for Janmashtami.

### Which to build first
1. **Raja Mantri Chor Sipahi**: tiny to build, made for online rooms, instant nostalgia.
2. **Pen Fight**: a 1v1 physics duel, very shareable.
3. **Kanche**: physics plus nostalgia, fun on touch screens.
4. **Book Cricket**: fast, India loves cricket, easy to share scores.

All of these reuse the FaceFlip base: rooms with a code or link, bots, same screen mode and the secure message checks.
